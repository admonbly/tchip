Privacy

In short: Tchip is played without an account, without ads and without tracking. Your progress stays on your device. Last updated: October 27, 2026.

What stays on your device

Your games, streak, stats, learned words, passport and settings are stored only in the app's memory on your phone (or in your browser). They are never sent to a server. If you delete the app, they're gone.

The daily expressions

To receive new expressions without an update, the app regularly downloads the public list of expressions, hosted on GitHub Pages. This request contains no game data. As with any web page, the host receives the technical IP address needed to respond.

The daily reminder

If you turn it on, the reminder is scheduled directly on your phone. It uses no server and no notification identifier. You can turn it off at any time in Settings.

Anonymous statistics

To find out whether people enjoy the game and to fix bugs, the app may send anonymous usage statistics to TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany, data hosted in the European Union):

  • app opened, game finished (with the score), share, reminder turned on;
  • technical errors (short message, no personal data);
  • platform (iOS, Android or web) and app version.

These events use a randomly generated identifier, hashed before sending and renewed every 13 months. It can't identify you and is never linked to another app or website. No advertising identifier is used. You can turn these statistics off in Settings › Anonymous statistics.

Suggesting an expression

If you suggest an expression, what you type in the form (the expression, the country, the city or region, the meanings, the example and, if you wish, a nickname) is sent to the Tchip team, along with your voice if you attach one. This data is stored by Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.) in the European Union, and only the team can access it. It isn't public and isn't linked to any account.

  • To block bots, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a real person is sending, using technical data from your browser or phone (see Cloudflare's privacy policy).
  • To limit abuse, we count submissions per device. Your IP address isn't stored: only a fingerprint that changes every day is kept, and it's deleted after 2 days.
  • A rejected suggestion is deleted after 90 days. An accepted suggestion goes into the game, with your nickname if you gave one.
  • Your voice is only published in the game if you agreed by ticking the box provided.
  • To have a suggestion deleted, write to contact@tchip.app with the expression and the date you sent it.

What Tchip doesn't do

  • No account, no email address or phone number requested.
  • No ads, no cross-app tracking, no selling of data.
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera.

Sharing your score, inviting friends

The “Share” and “Invite friends” buttons open your phone's share sheet. The shared text only contains your score and grid, or an invitation with the game's link, never any personal data. You choose who to send it to: Tchip doesn't read your address book.

Your rights

You can erase all your data at any time with Settings › Erase my data, or by deleting the app. For any question about your data or to exercise your rights (access, erasure, objection), write to contact@tchip.app. You can also contact the data protection authority in your country (in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).
